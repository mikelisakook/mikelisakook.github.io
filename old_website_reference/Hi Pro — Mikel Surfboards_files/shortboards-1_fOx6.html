Hi Pro

DD - The Designated Driver aka DD is essentially a squashed down version of a team rider Crowley’s favorite shortboard. Made with the goal in mind to fit a board in tighter east coast waves without loosing the performance.

The original started in a 5’5 which had glimpses of being complete magic. After working some kinks and cleaning up the outline we settled for a 5’7 that cut about 3 inches off his previous favorite.

Ridden with Drab fins

INT or Pivot

KIWI - Built from spending time between New Zealand and home on the east coast. Built off the all the good of the DD just making it a touch longer and a bit more relaxed in terms of rocker. Smooth consistent board I’ve loved as both a tri and quad with some subtle changes to the quad for a different style of surfing.

Ridden with Drab fins

Tri/ Quad
